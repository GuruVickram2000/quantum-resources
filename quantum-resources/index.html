WHO WE ARE

Built on Experience. Driven by Execution.

Quantum Resources was founded in 2025 by a team of professionals with extensive experience in supply chain management accumulated over three decades across various multinational corporations. The company operates across commodity trading, shipping and management consultancy assignments.

In India's rapidly growing economy, reliable access to energy, industrial materials and efficient supply chains is increasingly important. Quantum Resources combines commercial understanding, technical expertise and execution capability to support clients across multiple industrial sectors.
